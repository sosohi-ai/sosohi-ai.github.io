STAR appears in Findings of EMNLP 2026

Our paper, “STAR: Detecting Inference-Time Backdoors in LLM Reasoning via Prior-Posterior Amplification,” appears in Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2026.

The work studies inference-time backdoors in language-model reasoning and proposes a lightweight detector based on prior-posterior amplification.
